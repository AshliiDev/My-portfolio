About me

I graduated from PHINMA University of Pangasinan with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology, major in Cybersecurity. During my IT support internship at Concentrix, I got hands-on practice with hardware troubleshooting, RAM and monitor checks, diagnostic tools like Hiren’s BootCD, and installing software by following set procedures in a data center and workstation environment.

I have a working knowledge of networking fundamentals, including TCP/UDP, DNS and IP configuration, the OSI model, switch port diagnostics and LAN/WAN concepts. Through Cisco Networking Academy, I configure and secure networks in Packet Tracer, such as setting up a wireless router with DHCP and WPA2.

I work well with people and in a team, communicate clearly, and pick up new tools and processes quickly, so I’m easy to train. I stay organized by managing my schedule with to-do lists that track my progress, which keeps every task on track. Beyond IT support, I’m comfortable with productivity and multimedia tools, including Microsoft 365 for documents and organization, Figma for design, and video editing and Blender for multimedia work. I bring a technical background together with reliable, organized support.
